
# **Privacy Policy for FluentasAI SMS Services*

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

MicroHealth LLC ("MicroHealth," "we," "us," or "our") operates the FluentasAI platform accessible at https://ai.microhealthllc.com/ (the "Website," "Platform," or "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our SMS messaging capabilities, including our Send and SignFlow features, in full compliance with A2P 10DLC (Application-to-Person 10-Digit Long Code) regulations and applicable privacy laws.  This covers 2 features to send a one time passcode to decrypt a document sent by FluentasAI Send or perform document signatures with a one time passcode with FluentasAI Signflow.

**By using our SMS services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.**

- **Retention period: Minimum 4 years from consent date or last message sent**

## 5. A2P 10DLC Compliance

### 5.1 Consent Requirements
- We obtain **express written, verbal, or electronic consent** before sending SMS messages
- Consent is obtained through verified methods:
  - **Written Consent:** Signed forms or digital signatures
  - **Verbal/Phone Consent:** Via **(855) 294-3547** with documented agent ID and timestamp
  - **Mobile Application Consent:** In-app opt-in with clear disclosure
  - **Website Consent:** In-app opt-in with clear disclosure
- Users can **opt-out at any time** by replying "STOP" or calling **(855) 294-3547**
- We maintain **comprehensive records** of all consent and opt-out requests for **4+ years**
- We honor opt-out requests **within 24 hours**

### 5.2 Phone Consent Procedures
For consent obtained via phone at **(855) 294-3547**:
- Customer service representatives use the standardized script: *"May I send SMS updates to your number? You can reply STOP anytime."*
- Each consent interaction is logged with:
  - Agent identification number
  - Date and time stamp
  - Customer phone number
  - Call recording reference (where applicable)
  - Confirmation of customer's affirmative response

### 5.3 Message Content Standards
- We send only **authorized message types** registered with The Campaign Registry (TCR)
- Messages **clearly identify MicroHealth LLC** as the sender
- We do **not send prohibited content** including spam, phishing, hate speech, or illegal material
- All campaigns are **registered with TCR** before activation
- Messages include **opt-out instructions** (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe")
- Messages include **HELP keyword support** (Reply HELP for assistance)

### 5.4 Rate Limits and Throughput
- We comply with **carrier-imposed rate limits**
- Messages are sent according to **approved use cases**
- We maintain appropriate **message volume controls**
- We implement **throttling** to ensure compliance

### 5.5 Campaign Registration
- All SMS campaigns are **registered with The Campaign Registry (TCR)**
- We provide **accurate campaign information** including consent methods
- Campaign modifications require **re-registration**
- We maintain **proof of consent procedures** for verification
